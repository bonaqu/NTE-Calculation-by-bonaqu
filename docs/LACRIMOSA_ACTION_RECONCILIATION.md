## Why the Rotation Lab steps remain gaps

The preset says “transformation Redirect Skill,” “Basic Attacks one through five,” and “Redirect Skill to advance to the fifth Basic Attack.” It does not select:

1. Morning Tomato versus Devilish Gift, which copies an external enemy ability and has no fixed native damage ratio;
2. Tomato Metal versus Tomato Percussion for the full Basic string;
3. the melee versus ranged fifth attack after the Redirect Skill.

Therefore no exact binding is added. The three steps move from `missing-action-record` to `ambiguous-source-step`. The immutable 41-step baseline and the current 29-gap total remain unchanged.

- No animation seconds or complete-rotation DPS are inferred.
